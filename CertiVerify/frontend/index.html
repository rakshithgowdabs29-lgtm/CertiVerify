CertiVerify 5-Pillar Research Architecture

Detailed breakdown of the advanced AI and regulatory research innovations engineered into the platform.

01

Confidence-Aware Extraction

Extracts chemical assay values, heavy metal counts, and batch identifiers while estimating field-level confidence percentages for human verification.

02

Context-Aware Validation

Validates extracted values against authorized USP, EP, and ICH Q3D pharmacopeia monographs and threshold limits before deciding pass/fail status.

03

Cross-Document Consistency

Cross-examines data points across linked multi-document dossiers, such as matching Certificate of Analysis (CoA) records against Purchase Orders (POs).

04

Tamper Screening

Generates a unique SHA-256 cryptographic dossier hash seal for every audited batch, archiving immutable records in PostgreSQL[cite: 8].

05

Batch Anomaly Screening

Compares like-for-like batches against historical database records in PostgreSQL to flag unusual statistical drift or deviations for QA investigation[cite: 8].
